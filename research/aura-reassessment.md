# BCC Hacktoberfest: the LARP and aura reassessment

October 20, 2026. Two strong generalist engineers, three build days. This revisits all 17 concepts in the original research. It supersedes the earlier cross-track ranking; the underlying evidence and access checks in the original research remain useful.

## Revised decision

**Choose Block Ops, the reworked BlockBrief, for Track 3.** It has the strongest combination of audience participation, an immediately understandable role, real DC data, community control, and a credible three-day implementation.

**The Switch, the reworked DialectLens, is the higher-variance challenger.** It could have the bigger AI-specific stage moment, but must earn that recommendation with an actual, repeatable experiment tonight. We have not run its model evaluation.

**People's Chamber is the strongest performance-oriented alternate.** The Room is the most naturally interactive healthcare option; Reclaim retains the more distinctive healthcare mechanism. Reclaim drops from the overall first place because its honest ending is evidence prepared for review, while the new preference puts more value on operating, challenging and changing something in front of the room.

## What the new lens means

I interpret LARP positively: the judge inhabits a role and makes a consequential choice. Aura means recognizable identity, confidence, an understandable stake and a reveal people remember. These are design preferences, not new official judging criteria. The published rubric still requires track fit, creativity, functionality and presentation. [Official rubric](https://bcc-hacktoberfest.devpost.com/).

A strong interaction has four parts: the judge acts; the software actually computes or changes something; that change has an inspectable consequence; the result is useful after the demonstration ends. A fictional scenario is fine when labeled. Institutional authority, AI findings, successful healthcare outcomes and agency responses cannot be manufactured for the reveal.

The aura scores below are subjective stage-potential assessments out of five. They are not measured product quality, official scores or winning probabilities. A higher aura score can still lose on originality, evidence or build risk.

### The Switch: the bigger technical instrument

The judge becomes a community reviewer assessing an AI job-intake tool. One candidate has a fixed set of qualification facts. Two human-reviewed statements express those facts in African American English and mainstream English. The app runs the same task on both and links its factual claims to source words.

The central interaction is **Show me where**: a claim enters the applicant-approved record only when the reviewer can inspect and confirm support. This is more substantive than displaying two outputs and a fairness score.

An important technical control is rerunning the identical input. Compare differences between language versions with ordinary repeat-run instability. A changed output is not automatically a dialect effect. The app must also distinguish missing facts, contradictions and unsupported judgments rather than asking one LLM to certify another as fair.

**The stage sequence:** lock the meaning → throw the switch → challenge a claim → inspect the words → change the extraction policy → rerun → approve supported facts. The visual identity could be an editorial evidence studio with one tactile switch and prominent original language. The actual source-evidence gate supplies the power.

**Hard uncertainty:** we have not established a current model failure. A passing pair must pass honestly. A recorded failure must be labeled with its model/date and replay status. Injected checker-test errors cannot be presented as vendor outputs. An evidence-only prompt change does not, by itself, establish that a dialect-specific harm was fixed.

Bias testing is substantial prior art. The proposed distinction is the combination of locked meaning, repeat-input controls, understandable evidence challenges and a human release gate. [Nature research](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07856-5), [author code](https://github.com/valentinhofmann/dialect-prejudice), [Promptfoo race-bias testing](https://www.promptfoo.dev/docs/red-team/plugins/race-bias/).

**Two-person ceiling:** one intake task, a small reviewed fixture pack, two model adapters, structured facts, exact evidence spans, controls and export. Text first. Audio, arbitrary endpoints, avatars and a public incident network are stretch ideas that should not enter the three-day scope.

### The Room: the most participatory healthcare version

The user chooses what they want to communicate: finish a concern, ask for a plain-language explanation or clarify a next step. They practice a short, clearly fictional exchange, can pause and retry, and choose how a supporter should help. Their original words and unanswered questions survive into an editable visit card.

The stage action is a judge selecting the goal and a retry branch, followed by the second engineer taking the agreed supporter role. The app genuinely changes the rehearsal and resulting card. Two scenarios and three turns are enough. Text input is a complete mode; voice is optional.

The role is patient/supporter communication practice. It does not require a medical persona, medical advice, diagnosis, symptom scoring or grading someone's accent/confidence. Public participants use invented scenarios rather than disclose their own health history.

**Why it still loses the overall recommendation:** rehearsal and patient advocacy already have direct competitors. A freshly branded AI doctor to practice with is weak differentiation. The proposed patient-goal, supporter-consent and original-language workflow remains an unvalidated design hypothesis. [Yoodli roleplay documentation](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/11565137-how-to-build-and-customize-roleplays), [HEARD patient-advocacy features](https://www.myheardapp.com/patient-advocacy-app). The track researcher also inspected [Tender Health Practice Room](https://www.tenderhealth.ca/practice-room), which advertises patient rehearsal; the parent fetch timed out, so no additional capability claim is made here.

### Why Reclaim drops without becoming a bad idea

Reclaim has a strong source-backed numerical reveal and a distinctive historical mechanism. Its useful ending remains a record-review packet for a transplant program. Increasing interactivity can improve it, but the software cannot provide the most dramatic imagined ending: restored waiting time or a changed place in line. Those require the actual transplant process.

Keep same-specimen values, source inspection, explicit uncertainty and user confirmation. Treat an unrelated old-high/new-low pairing as an important refusal case. Do not turn patient illness into a game or a simulated institutional victory. The original healthcare research retains its evidence and exact policy boundaries.

### Block Ops: the version to lock in

**Pitch:** “Block Ops gives a neighborhood group a shared case table: what was reported, what the records actually say, what residents observe now, and what we choose to do next.”

The role is a community-run organizing room. People act as neighbor, records keeper and meeting speaker. The useful power is control over the group's own evidence and next action. This is a service-follow-through and organizing tool; it does not dispatch city crews or investigate residents.

**One real mechanic earns the entire premise:** a judge rejects an unrelated record. The timeline, counts, supporting evidence and final agency question must all change. If the export stays the same, the interaction is only theater.

The source panel keeps official status separate from a dated resident account. A closed ticket is one recorded fact. A resident reporting that a condition is present is another, attributed to that resident. A nearby or repeated address suggests a candidate relationship; a person still reviews it.

**The 90-second demo:**

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–15 sec | Open a dated DC issue history. The group has a meeting and needs a clear account. |
| 15–35 sec | Hand the judge the records-keeper role. They exclude a questionable record; the evidence changes. |
| 35–50 sec | The second engineer adds an explicitly labeled fictional resident observation. Official status and observation remain separate. |
| 50–70 sec | The judge selects a concrete next step, such as asking for a closure explanation, and orders two follow-up tasks. |
| 70–90 sec | Export and open the actual issue brief and small action plan. Show the effect of the judge's choice. |

**Visual direction:** a civic workshop table with large request IDs, stamped dates, legible evidence cards and a confident color system. Motion follows record removal, confirmation and plan changes. A source-backed case table can carry the identity without building 3D streets or a wall of empty analytics.

**What actually runs:** the data load or dated snapshot, reviewed group membership, derived counts/dates, observation entry, edited question, local action cards and export. Participant roles and current-observation scenarios are explicitly fictional in the demo. No city response or cleanup outcome is implied.

**Scope:** one ward, one issue category and one complete case workflow. One laptop is enough. The two engineers can hand roles to the judge on the same screen. Multiuser networking, automatic complaint delivery, real-time voice and city integrations are unnecessary for the core interaction.

### Fresh public-data check

The parent researcher ran a new unauthenticated read-only query against the official 2026 DC311 layer. Filters: Ward 8, category containing “Dump,” dates from July 1 inclusive to October 20 exclusive, UTC; selected fields only; maximum 1,000 rows. The API returned **475 distinct Illegal Dumping request IDs**, with no transfer-limit truncation. Normalizing the address and grouping by address/category yielded **68 candidate groups containing multiple requests**. One candidate group contained five requests on five distinct dates from July 14 to September 18; all five had official status Closed. [Official data layer and metadata](https://maps2.dcgis.dc.gov/dcgis/rest/services/DCGIS_DATA/ServiceRequests/FeatureServer/21?f=pjson).

These are calculations from the query, not city-published summary statistics. The groups are useful material to inspect. They do not establish that the requests concern the same incident, that any closure was wrong, that a condition persists, or that this location was treated inequitably. Full manual case review remains a build step. The endpoint, record cap and pagination had also been checked in the earlier research.

Prior art still matters: official 311 interfaces, DC's To-Do List and SeeClickFix already provide reporting/dashboard functions. The proposed distinction is the reviewed evidence-to-action workflow. [Existing DC dashboard](https://viridicity.github.io/dc311/), [SeeClickFix duplicate workflow](https://www.civicservice.civicplus.help/hc/en-us/articles/360043293074-Mark-a-Request-as-a-Duplicate).

### People's Chamber: the best pure stage alternative

A resident gets the floor; a judge plays a practice chair; one question causes the resident to revise their own ask. The timer, document context, revision and testimony export function for real. A large floor card and visible source notice supply the visual identity. A live human playing chair is enough; there is no need for an AI official avatar.

Its risk is becoming a generic testimony writer with a timer. The revised ask, source separation and participant control have to be central. Public notices support an upload-first path, so LIMS API approval is not a dependency. The actual hearing notice controls submission instructions. [DC Council testimony process](https://dccouncil.gov/how-to-testify-before-the-council/).

## Tonight's decision and three-day ceiling

My default decision is **Block Ops**. Spend tonight selecting and manually inspecting one source history and sketching the case-table interaction. Do not wait for a dramatic AI failure before committing to a build.

If the team strongly prefers The Switch, give it one fixed 45–60 minute feasibility experiment tonight: reviewed same-meaning inputs, a realistic extraction task, identical-input repeat controls and held-out examples. A material, repeatable omission or unsupported inference can justify continuing. A dialect-specific claim requires credible equivalence review and comparison against ordinary instability. Do not spend Wednesday fishing for an inflammatory output. This experiment has not been run in this research.

For Block Ops, Wednesday must end with the complete source → selection → edited ask → export path. Thursday adds the judge interaction, source inspection, false-relationship handling and visual identity. Friday is correctness checks, task walkthroughs, presentation, transparent backup recording and submission. Keep the Friday-night deadline in the original brief.

Do not merge the 17 concepts into a platform. The project should let one person exercise one meaningful form of control and leave with one useful result. That is how the LARP becomes an operating interaction and the aura survives a judge clicking the buttons.
